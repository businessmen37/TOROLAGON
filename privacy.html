Privacy Policy

How TOROLAGON LLC handles information provided through this website.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Information we collect

When ordering or contacting us, you may provide information such as your name, email address, shipping address, order details, and customer-service communications.

How we use information

We use customer information to process orders, provide shipping and tracking updates, respond to support requests, prevent fraud, maintain business records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Checkout and payment information

When online payment processing is enabled, payment transactions will be handled by the payment provider connected to the store. TOROLAGON does not intend to store full payment-card numbers on this static website.

Order emails

The email address provided at checkout is used for transactional communications including order confirmation, shipping updates, tracking information, delivery information, refunds, returns, and important service messages. Promotional marketing, if introduced later, should use a separate opt-in process.

Browser storage

This website may use local browser storage to remember items placed in your shopping cart. You can clear this information through your browser settings.

Service providers

We may use third-party providers for website hosting, payments, shipping, analytics, email delivery, and other business operations. These providers may receive information needed to perform their services.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for order fulfillment, customer service, accounting, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

Your choices

You may contact info@torolagon.com with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have provided to us, subject to applicable law and required business record retention.
