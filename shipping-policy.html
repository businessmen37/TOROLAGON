Shipping Policy

Current shipping terms for TOROLAGON LLC orders.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Shipping area

TOROLAGON LLC currently accepts and ships orders only within the United States.

Shipping cost

Standard shipping is free on current U.S. orders.

Processing time

Orders are normally processed within 24 to 48 hours. Processing time is separate from carrier transit time.

Tracking

Customers must provide a valid email address at checkout. Order confirmation, shipping confirmation, tracking information, and other important order updates are sent to that email address.

Delivery delays

Carrier delays can occur after an order leaves our control. If tracking shows an unusual delay, contact info@torolagon.com and we will help review the available tracking information.

Incorrect addresses

Customers are responsible for entering a complete and accurate shipping address. Contact us as soon as possible if an address needs correction. We cannot guarantee changes after an order has entered fulfillment.

Lost or damaged packages

If an order arrives damaged, or tracking indicates a delivery problem, email info@torolagon.com with the order number and relevant photos or tracking details.
